\chapter{Analysis of Recent Attacks}
\label{ch:AnalysisRecentAttacks}

In this chapter, we present 
in chronological order (from the oldest to the most recent)
the four relevant \acp{SSCA} 
that occurred in 2025 in the \ac{NPM} ecosystem, 
which we selected as case studies.

We analyze each of these attacks in detail:
how many packages they compromised,
how they work and what their main characteristics are, 
and how they differ from their benign versions.
